Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 19, 2026

HagimiMonitor ("the App") takes your privacy seriously. The App is built on a simple principle: all data stays on your device. We do not collect, store, or transmit any personal information.

We Collect No Data

The App is a local system-monitoring tool. All metrics — CPU, GPU, memory, storage, network, and battery — are read and displayed locally on your Mac in real time. They are never sent to any server, logged, or stored.

No Tracking or Analytics

The App contains no third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs. We do not collect usage behavior, device identifiers, or any information that could identify you.

Network Access

The App accesses the network only in the following case:

  • Update checks: The App connects to public GitHub release information over a secure HTTPS connection to check whether a new version is available. This only retrieves public data such as version numbers and sends none of your personal data.

Data Storage

The App's preferences (such as theme and visible modules) are stored locally on your device using standard macOS mechanisms. They are never synced or uploaded.

Children's Privacy

The App is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone.

Changes to This Policy

If this Privacy Policy changes, we will update this page and revise the "Last updated" date above.

Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please reach out via our GitHub project page.
